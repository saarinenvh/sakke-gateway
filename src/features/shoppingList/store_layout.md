The shopping list's store order. Each `##` heading is a section, in the order
you walk through the store. Under it, the words that put an item there,
separated by commas. An item goes to the section of the longest word its name
contains ("ice cream" beats "cream"); an item matching none sorts last.

The gateway reads `home/context/store_layout.md` from the wiki, and this
bundled copy when the wiki has none.
